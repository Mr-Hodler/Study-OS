Study OS — how to actually use it

Two skills, one Notion database, and one test between them. If the change lands inside a page's chapters it is study-os. If it lands between pages, in the metadata, the links, the index or the stubs, it is study-librarian. Organised by the job you are doing. For everything about one skill open its SKILL.md; for the rules it enforces, open the file under references/ that governs the thing you are about to do.

What every skill here does, whatever it is about

Writing standardsstudy-os/references/writing-standards.md
Open it before writing any page. Voice, the soul rules, the signpost system, the em dash ban, the decision rule between tables, bullets, columns and diagrams, and the Page Purpose, Non-scope and Related discipline.
Voicemethodical, evidence-based, active, maximum information density. Concise is not shallow
Soula correct page that reads like a generated template is a failure. Open on a hook, never on This chapter covers
Vary the shapedo not repeat one Summary and Content skeleton per chapter. A comparison becomes a table, a debate becomes two short positions
SignpostsKey idea, Example, Analogy, Insight, Watch out, Go deeper, used consistently as inline bold labels
Chaptersreal H2 headings, never toggles. Toggles are for minor and appendix content only
Linksembedded in descriptive text, no bare URLs, and no dotted domain in the visible label: Notion auto-links it into a broken double link
Em dashnever, in any output, in any mode. Verbatim quoted source text is the only exception
Page architecturestudy-os/references/page-architecture.md
Open it when you are about to emit blocks. The skeleton in order, what a strong chapter contains, and the exact Notion-flavored syntax for callouts, tables, columns, mentions, the table of contents and toggles.
The skeletonmeta callout, purpose callout, intro hook, sub-pages index near the top, topic heading, rule, table of contents, chapters, then Glossary, Resources, Sources
Deep divesthe sub-page cards are the index. Do not add a table beside them repeating the same titles
A strong chapterhook, key idea, substance shaped to the material, a concrete or numeric example, a misconception where one exists, go deeper
Use the mediumtables for comparisons and matrices, columns for two parallel blocks, callouts for what must stand out. A page of only bullets is under-built
Arrowsavoid arrow notation in Notion text, the bracket gets escaped. Write then, or draw a real diagram
Research protocolstudy-os/references/research-protocol.md
Open it before the research pass. The depth bar, the source hierarchy, absolute dating, the uncertainty formula, and the index confirmation that precedes writing. This is what separates the output from shallow AI writing.
Depth barA to Z, including the trade-offs, the edge cases and the state of the art. If an expert would ask but what about X, X is addressed
Source orderprimary sources and official documentation, then standards bodies and peer-reviewed or industry references, then reputable secondary analysis
Datingabsolute dates in the citation for prices, regulations, versions, market figures, leadership and any latest claim
Search, do not recallpresent-day facts are searched. Facts drift, and memory does not know that it has
UncertaintyUncertainty: [what is unclear] / Reduce via: [what would resolve it]. Never fabricate a statistic, a quote or a citation
Before writingthe chapter index and the source list, confirmed, unless autonomous mode was requested
Notion operationsstudy-os/references/notion-operations.md
Open it for any read or write against Notion. Schema detection, which write call to use and why one of them is dangerous, the property-writing quirks, and the knowledge-graph links.
Read firstfetch the page, then the database, then the collection://<data_source_id> from its data-source tag. Property names come from there
Write surgicallyinsert_content to append, update_content for a targeted replace. A new page is written in a single create call
The dangerreplace_content flags child pages and attachments for deletion unless every one is re-included in the new body
Property quirkscheckboxes take __YES__ and __NO__, numbers are numbers, id and url need the userDefined: prefix, dates split into start, end and is_datetime
Render trapafter a page card, start the next block with a heading or a paragraph. A bare list item gets absorbed into the card area
Verifyre-fetch after any large edit and confirm the result rendered as intended
Classificationstudy-os/references/classification.md
Open it when you have to decide how a page is written. Theory against Practice against Real Setup, the ten-second test, hybrid pages, and the anti-duplication rule.
The testwhy or how to think is Theory. How do I do it, step by step is Practice. How is my system set up right now is Real Setup
Practice writinglink the best official guide rather than rewriting it, especially for install, operational or security detail that must stay current
Real Setupout of scope for the study library. It lives in a separate ops space, and the page references where
Hybridtwo explicit sections on one page, concepts and criteria, then procedure and checklist
Anti-duplicationwhere a sibling covers part of it, use the Non-scope and Related lines rather than repeating the content
Media and assetsstudy-os/references/media-assets.md
Open it when a visual is needed. The decision table across Mermaid, image by URL, generated file and video, the image-slot and handoff patterns, and the hotlinking reality check.
Mermaid firstflowcharts, sequences, state, ER, mindmap, timeline, pie. It renders natively, so a diagram that can be Mermaid must be
The URL trapmany hosts block hotlinking, so the image block is created and renders blank. Wikimedia in particular often does not render
Generated filefor a precise chart: generate it, present it, and mark the exact spot with a slot naming the filename and what it shows

Output targetsstudy-os/references/output-targets.md
Open it when the target is a PDF or a document rather than Notion. The shared content model and the element-by-element mapping. Structure, depth and formatting are identical across targets; only the rendering mechanics change.
Mappinga chapter becomes a clear section listed in a table of contents, callouts become a boxed intro block, an appendix toggle becomes a labeled appendix
Choosinga Notion URL or update the page means Notion; PDF, printable or hand this to someone means a document. Default is Notion
Known conflictthis file says chapters are toggle headings and describes every chapter as Summary bullets plus Content. page-architecture.md, writing-standards.md and qa-review.md all require real H2 headings and reject one repeated skeleton. Follow the three, not this one
Reformat modestudy-os/references/reformat-mode.md
Open it when running Reformat. Text invariance, what may and may not change, and the one place the no-em-dash rule yields to it.
Invarianceno word, punctuation mark, number, emoji or URL is added, removed or modified. Only block types and whitespace
Scopeif a selection is present, nothing outside it is touched
Order of workline breaks and blocks, then bullets and checklists, then headings, then toggles, callouts, columns and tables
Tablesonly where the text is already table-like and the mapping is unambiguous. No cell text, capitalization or link changes
The exceptionan existing em dash is left in place and flagged, because removing it would change punctuation
QA reviewstudy-os/references/qa-review.md
Open it before presenting anything. The final gate, run in full, then fix what failed and re-fetch to confirm the page rendered.
Deptha key idea and at least one concrete, often numeric, example per chapter; the hard points expanded rather than glossed
Structurethe sub-pages index near the top, chapters as real headings, the shape varying per chapter
VisualsMermaid where Mermaid is possible, attribution on any embedded image, a slot where a generated asset belongs
Sourcesclaims, data, quotes and contested points cited, volatile facts dated, nothing fabricated
Stylezero em dashes outside verbatim quotes: scan and remove. Consistent signposts, bold label before a colon
Finalre-fetch and confirm tables, columns, Mermaid and page cards actually rendered
Library operationsstudy-librarian/references/library-operations.md
Open it for any Librarian mode. Reading the library, metadata hygiene, the two graph layers, deduplication, the map page, and the scaffolding procedure.
Read the libraryfetch the database and its data source, enumerate the rows and child pages, then build the picture of titles, metadata and who links to whom
Hygienecategory from existing options, scope, reading order coherent across siblings, a sharp one or two line description, page URL
Two graph layersthe Prerequisites self-relation for ordered dependency, and Related lines with inline page mentions for adjacency
Never changeStatus and Next review. They are the user's learning state, out of scope
Dedupea merge proposal for a genuine duplicate, a scope split for partial overlap. Confirmation first, child pages and attachments preserved
Scaffoldget the plan, confirm the structure, create the hub plus one empty stub per item with metadata and prerequisites, then hand off to study-os
Audit checkliststudy-librarian/references/audit-checklist.md
Open it when running Audit. The per-page structure, hygiene and metadata checks, the library-level checks, and the report format. Note that several checks here are defined by the two Study OS standards files, so a Librarian installed on its own does not ship the rules it audits against.
Per pagemeta and purpose callouts, chapters as real headings, a Sources section where claims appear, consistent signposts
Hygieneno em dashes outside quotes, no bare URLs or dotted-domain labels, no blank image boxes, no leftover placeholders
Metadatacategory, scope, reading order, description, page URL. Status and Next review are neither flagged nor changed
Library levelorphans, duplicates, reading-order gaps and collisions, an acyclic prerequisite graph, hub cards that still resolve, and the obvious topic gaps
The reportblockers first, then should-fix, then nice-to-have, each with the page link and the one-line fix, closing with an offer to run Organize and to hand content gaps to study-os

What counts as knowing something

Every statement is tagged
Key ideathe core insight, in one or two linestext is split into subword fragments mapped to integer IDs, then to learned vectors
Examplea concrete, real example or mini-scenario, often numeric1 token is about 0.75 English words, so a 1,000-word memo is roughly 1,300 tokens
Analogya familiar comparison for an abstract or technical idea. One good analogy saves three paragraphsa lock and key, a tree, a sealed envelope
Insighta sharp, memorable rule of thumb
Watch outa common mistake, risk or misconception
Open questionwhat is unsettled or at the frontier, added for meaty topics
Go deeperthe sources and the deep-dive sub-pageBPE, Sennrich et al., 2016
Every claim carries a weight
1primary sources and official documentation
2standards bodies, peer-reviewed and industry references
3reputable secondary analysis
nolow-quality or SEO-spam pages, and anything recalled rather than checked

Who owns which object

One owner per object. A skill needing something it does not own reads the identifier and attaches to it, rather than minting a second one for the same thing.

Anything describing a set of records is computed

A field describing other records diverges within a week when typed by hand, and a hand-filled rollup is noise that looks like signal — worse than an empty field, because nobody knows it is wrong.

AUDIT SEVERITY           per finding, reported and never fixed in place
  blocker · should-fix · nice-to-have       ← each with the page link and a one-line fix

ORPHAN                   a page with no links in and none out
  reachable                     through a category, a prerequisite, or a related link

GAP                      a topic a complete library on this subject would have and does not
  reported beside the orphans, as a first-class finding rather than an afterthought

READING ORDER            coherent across siblings: no gaps, no collisions within a category or series

PREREQUISITE GRAPH       acyclic, and matching the read-after notes in the page intros

DUPLICATE                heavy overlap across titles, descriptions, categories and chapter headings
  merge proposed                naming which page to keep
  scope split                   written through the Non-scope and Related lines
                                ← both act only on confirmation, and preserve child pages and attachments

Who reads what

Different people arrive with different questions. Nobody should have to read a document written for someone else.

The minimum that still works

Not a beginner mode. This is what stays true at every size; everything else is an addition a specific question makes necessary.

What to add, and what triggers it

Add a field when the question is asked, not in anticipation. And remove one that stopped being read.

What breaks it

Every term this suite uses, in one line, grouped by where it lives. The words are not interchangeable — most confusion comes from treating two of them as the same object.
